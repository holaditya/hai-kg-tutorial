Element Categories

When analyzing StarCraft II screenshots, we categorize game elements based on their strategic importance and urgency

Good

Routine or positive situations supporting your strategy.

  • Workers mining resources normally
  • Completed defensive structures
  • Successful tech upgrades
  • Stable economy
Warning

Situations requiring attention but not immediate action.

  • Enemy scouts in your territory
  • Low on a particular resource
  • Defensive gaps
  • Enemy building military units
Critical

Urgent situations requiring immediate action.

  • Direct attacks on your base
  • Running out of essential resources
  • Key structures under threat
  • Major strategic disadvantages
Relationship Types

Relationships describe how game elements interact and affect each other strategically.

is enabling
Currently powering or supporting another element Supply Depot is enabling→ Army The Supply Depot provides supply that allows army units to exist. Pylon is enabling→ Protoss Buildings The Pylon provides power required for nearby Protoss structures to function.
could enable
Has potential to help, but is not doing so right now Idle Worker could enable→ Resource Gathering The idle worker can start mining if assigned to minerals or gas.
is blocking
Directly preventing another element from functioning Supply Block is blocking→ Unit Production A supply block prevents unit production: units cannot be produced until available supply is increased.
could block
Could become a constraint in the future Enemy Army could block→ Expansion The enemy army's nearby presence can deny or delay new base construction.
has no impact on
No meaningful effect on the other element right now Defensive Turret has no impact on→ Mineral Gathering The turret does not change worker mining rate or assignment.
Example Scenario

A sample StarCraft II situation and its strategic representation.

Situation

You are playing as Terran. Your base has Marines defending, but enemy Zerglings are approaching. Your Command Center is low on health from a previous attack.

Game Elements
Marines (Good) - Your defensive units are ready
Command Center (Critical) - Low health, needs immediate protection
Enemy Zerglings (Critical) - Immediate threat near your base
Relationships
Marines is enabling Command Center
Marines are actively intercepting incoming Zerglings, reducing direct damage to the Command Center and buying time for repairs or reinforcements.
Enemy Zerglings could block Command Center
The Zerglings are already pressuring the Command Center, so continued damage can force worker pullbacks, interrupt mining, and delay repairs or production decisions.
Marines could block Enemy Zerglings
If focused correctly, Marines can eliminate the Zerglings before they overwhelm the position, reducing immediate threat and restoring control of the fight.
